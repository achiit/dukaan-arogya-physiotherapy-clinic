Physiotherapist and founder, Aarogya Physio

Why I opened a clinic in my own town

My mama ji had a slip disc and used to travel hours for treatment, sitting on a bus with a bad back. That was the day I decided Bijainagar needed its own proper physiotherapy clinic.

I started Aarogya Physio a few years ago. Since then I have treated farmers with back pain, players with torn ligaments and dadi-nani who could not climb stairs. Every patient gets my time, not a number.

I explain your problem in Hindi or Marwari, in simple words, so you know exactly what we are fixing.

  • Exercise-based treatment, not only machines
  • Doctor treats you himself at every visit
  • Home exercise plan so you recover faster
  • Care for elders, with mobility and balance training
  • Open Sunday also, for those who work all week
